📚 The strategies you can practice

📉 RSI Swing · Dip default bot

Buy weakness: when the 1h RSI drops to / below 30 (oversold) and you hold nothing. The original CrytpTrde strategy.

🚀 RSI Swing · Momentum family 2

Buy strength: when the 1h RSI rises to / above 55 (breakout). Good in trending markets; the second family in the 500-bot tournament.

🎯 Take-Profit

Sell once price is +4% above your average cost. Locks in gains instead of waiting for the top.

🛡️ Stop-Loss

Sell once price is −2% below average cost. Caps the damage on a wrong call. Same engine for both bot families.

🔥 RSI Overbought Exit

Sell early when the 1h RSI climbs to 72 — the coin looks toppy, take profits before the swing back.

⏳ Hold-Period Bot

Exit on a schedule: sell after the position has been held N hours (e.g. 168h = 1 week, 720h = 1 month), price be damned.
